Things you probably wonder

How does Roomify ensure call security and privacy?

All video and audio streams are protected with 256-bit AES end-to-end encryption. Your session data and AI summaries are strictly private and never used to train external models.

Do my guests need to install an app to join a call?

No. Guests can join directly through any modern web browser on desktop or mobile with a single click on your meeting link, without creating an account or installing anything.

How does the AI Companion transcribe meetings in real time?

Roomify AI listens in the background, attributing speech to individual speakers and generating live captions and structured minutes that can be exported directly to Slack, Notion, or PDF.

Can I record meetings and share highlights with my team?

Yes. You can record locally or to Roomify Cloud in 1080p. Our Smart Clips tool allows you to trim key moments and share password-protected snippets with anyone.

What operating systems and devices are supported?

Roomify is fully cross-platform with native desktop apps for Windows, macOS (Apple Silicon & Intel), Linux, and mobile apps for iOS and Android, plus our full-featured Web Client.
